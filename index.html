About Niche

Niche Marketing Management is a Dubai-based field marketing company. For more than 20 years our teams have audited shelves, merchandised stores, run promotions and scored showrooms for the world's biggest brands, from one key contact and one invoice.

Privately owned and headquartered in Dubai, with branches across the Middle East, North Africa and Asia and warehousing in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.
