Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated 9 November 2026. This notice explains what personal data this website collects, why, and how to reach us about it.

What we collect

When you submit the proposal brief or a job application on this site, we collect the details you enter: your name, company (for a brief), email address, phone number, the services or role you are interested in, the countries or markets involved, and anything you write in the message field. The careers form also collects a LinkedIn or CV link if you provide one. We do not collect this data unless you submit one of these forms.

How it is used

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry or application: to prepare a proposal, to get in touch about a role, or to answer a question. We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for marketing unrelated to your enquiry.

Who sees it

Submissions are delivered by email to the relevant Niche team using Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service that relays the submission from your browser to our inbox. Web3Forms processes the data only to deliver that one email; see their privacy policy for how they handle it in transit. No other third party receives this data.

Recruitment fees

Niche never asks applicants to pay a fee, deposit or any form of payment at any stage of recruitment. If anyone contacts you claiming to represent Niche and asks for payment, it is not us — please report it and contact us directly at the address below to verify.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application data for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and, for applications, to consider you for a suitable role. You can ask us to delete your data at any time using the contact details below.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent for us to hold it, by emailing info@nicheme.com.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a small amount of data in your browser (such as whether you have seen the intro animation, or your chosen filters) to make the site work smoothly; this stays on your device and is never sent to us.
